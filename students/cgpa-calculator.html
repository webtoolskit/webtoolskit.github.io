What is this Tool?

The CGPA Calculator helps students calculate their Cumulative Grade Point Average from semester-wise grades and credit hours. CGPA is the weighted average of all grade points earned across all semesters, where each course's grade point is weighted by its credit hours. This tool supports the 10-point grading system used by most Indian universities including IITs, NITs, and affiliated colleges.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How is CGPA calculated?
A: CGPA = Sum of (SGPA × Credits for each semester) ÷ Total Credits across all semesters. Each semester's SGPA is weighted by its credit load.
Q: How do I convert CGPA to percentage?
A: Most Indian universities use: Percentage = CGPA × 9.5. Some universities use CGPA × 10. Check your university's official conversion formula as it varies.
Q: What CGPA is required for placements?
A: Most top companies require a minimum CGPA of 6.0 or 7.0. Premium companies and PSUs often require 7.5 or above. Government jobs through UPSC/SSC don't use CGPA.
Q: What is the difference between SGPA and CGPA?
A: SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) is your GPA for a single semester. CGPA (Cumulative GPA) is the weighted average across all completed semesters.
💡 Pro Tip: Even if your early semester CGPA is low, it can be significantly improved by consistently scoring high in later semesters — especially if later semesters have higher credit loads. Don't give up after a bad semester.
