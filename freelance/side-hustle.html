What is this Tool?

The Side Hustle Income Calculator helps you estimate the monthly and annual earning potential of your side business based on your rates, available hours, and expected growth. Frequently Asked Questions

How many hours should I invest in a side hustle?
Start with 5-10 hours per week while maintaining your primary job. This is sustainable long-term. 20+ hours per week risks burnout unless you're planning to transition full-time soon.
What are the best side hustles in India?
Freelance writing, graphic design, web development, digital marketing, online tutoring, video editing, social media management, and consulting are consistently high-demand and well-paying side hustles.
When should I go full-time?
Most financial advisors suggest transitioning when your side income covers 6 months of expenses as savings AND consistently earns 75-100% of your current salary for at least 3-6 months.
Do I need to pay taxes on side hustle income?
Yes. In India, all income — including freelance and side hustle income — is taxable. Keep records of all income and expenses. Consider registering as a sole proprietor for easier tax filing and to claim business expense deductions.
How much can I earn from a side hustle?
It depends on your rate and the time you can commit. At ₹500 per hour for 10 hours a week, you'd earn about ₹20,000 a month or ₹2.4 lakh a year before expenses. Enter your own numbers above for a realistic estimate.
What are good side hustles in India?
Popular options include freelance writing, graphic design, social media management, tutoring, resume writing, virtual assistance, video editing and selling digital products like ebooks or templates. The best one uses a skill you already have.
Can a salaried employee do freelancing in India?
Legally there's no blanket ban, but your employment contract may restrict outside work or require permission (often called a moonlighting clause). Read your contract and avoid working for competitors or using company time or equipment.
Is side hustle income taxable in India?
Yes. Side income is added to your total income and taxed at your slab rate. Under the new tax regime for FY 2026-27, total income up to ₹12 lakh is effectively tax-free because of the rebate. If your tax due for the year exceeds ₹10,000, you may need to pay advance tax.
How many hours a week should I spend on a side hustle?
Most people can sustain 5–15 hours a week alongside a full-time job. Start small, keep it consistent and increase hours only when the income justifies it.
How do I start a side hustle with no money?
Start with a service based on a skill you already have — writing, design, tutoring, editing — since it needs no stock or upfront cost. Free tools and AI assistants can help you produce faster while you build your first few clients.
Can I use AI to make money on the side?
Yes. AI tools can speed up writing, research, design drafts and content planning, which lets you take on more work in less time. The value clients pay for is still your judgement, editing and understanding of their needs.
How do I price my side hustle services?
Use our Freelance Rate Calculator to work out a rate that covers your income goal, expenses and tax. Beginners can start slightly lower to win first clients, then raise rates as reviews and experience build up.
Should I charge per hour or per project for side work?
Per-project pricing often suits side hustles because clients don't have to track your hours and you benefit from working faster. Use hourly billing for ongoing tasks with changing scope.
When should I turn my side hustle into full-time work?
A common benchmark is when your side income consistently covers most of your essential expenses for several months, and you have savings to cover gaps. Check how your notice period affects the switch with our Notice Period Calculator.
💡 Pro Tip: The fastest way to scale a side hustle is to raise your rates, not just work more hours. Doubling your rate and working the same hours doubles your income without doubling your time investment.
