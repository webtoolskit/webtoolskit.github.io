What is this Tool?

The Word and Character Counter is an essential writing tool that tracks words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, and estimated reading time in real time as you type or paste text. Whether you're writing a tweet, crafting an Instagram caption, drafting an email, or checking the length of a blog post, this tool ensures your content fits the platform's requirements perfectly.

How to Use This Tool

1
Type or paste your text into the editor.
2
See word count, character count, sentences, paragraphs, and reading time update in real time.
3
Click a platform button (Twitter, Threads, Instagram, LinkedIn, SMS) to set a character limit.
4
A visual progress bar shows how close you are to the limit.
5
When you exceed the limit, the bar turns red as a warning.

What is the ideal blog post length for SEO?
For SEO, aim for 1,500–2,500 words for informational posts. Longer content (2,500+ words) tends to rank better for competitive keywords. However, quality matters more than length — don't pad content unnecessarily.
How many words per minute is the average reading speed?
The average adult reads approximately 200–250 words per minute silently. This calculator uses 200 WPM as the baseline. For audio content, the average speaking pace is 130–150 words per minute.
What is the ideal email length?
Research suggests emails between 50–125 words get the highest response rates. Marketing emails perform best at 200–500 words. Long emails (1,000+ words) should use clear headings and bullet points for scannability.
Does Twitter count spaces and punctuation?
Yes, every character including spaces, punctuation, and emojis counts toward Twitter's 280-character limit. URLs are automatically shortened to 23 characters regardless of actual length.

How Many Words is X Characters?

How many words is 140 characters?
140 characters is approximately 20–25 words. This is the original Twitter character limit, ideal for a single punchy sentence or a short announcement.
How many words is 255 characters?
255 characters is approximately 40–50 words — roughly 3–4 short sentences. This is a common limit for meta descriptions, SMS messages, and database fields.
How many words is 280 characters?
280 characters is approximately 45–55 words. This is the current Twitter/X character limit — enough for a tweet with a short sentence and a hashtag or two.
How many words is 300 characters?
300 characters is approximately 45–55 words — around 3–5 sentences. A 300-character paragraph is common for short product descriptions, SMS campaigns, and push notifications.
How many words is 500 characters?
500 characters is approximately 75–100 words — around 5–7 sentences. This is the Threads post character limit and a common length for short app store descriptions.
How many words is 750 characters?
750 characters is approximately 110–130 words — roughly a short paragraph or a LinkedIn post intro. At average typing speed, this takes about 1–2 minutes to write.
How many words is 1,000 characters?
1,000 characters is approximately 150–170 words — about half a standard A4 page of text. This is a common limit for college application short answers and review platforms.
How many words is 2,000 characters?
2,000 characters is approximately 300–350 words — roughly one full A4 page double-spaced. This is a typical word count for a short blog intro or a 1-minute speech.
How many words is 2,300 characters?
2,300 characters is approximately 350–400 words. This is a common length for a 250-word college essay (which typically runs around 1,500 characters) or a detailed product review.
How many words is 2,500 characters?
2,500 characters is approximately 380–430 words — around 3–4 short paragraphs. This length is typical for a detailed FAQ answer or a short news article intro.
How many words is 8,000 characters?
8,000 characters is approximately 1,200–1,400 words — roughly a 5–6 minute read. This is a common length for a detailed blog post, a short essay, or a feature article.
How many words is 9,000 characters?
9,000 characters is approximately 1,350–1,550 words — around a 6–7 minute read. This is a good length for an in-depth how-to guide or a detailed opinion piece.
How many sentences is 140 characters?
140 characters is typically 1–2 short sentences. At an average of 15–20 words per sentence, you can fit one complete thought or two very brief clauses.
How many sentences is 280 characters?
280 characters is typically 2–3 sentences. This is enough for a tweet with context — a statement, a qualifier, and a call to action.
How many sentences is 750 characters?
750 characters is approximately 5–7 sentences. This is a comfortable length for a short comment, a Glassdoor review, or a brief product testimonial.
How many sentences is 1,500 characters?
1,500 characters is approximately 10–12 sentences — about two short paragraphs. This is a common length for an email response, a LinkedIn comment, or a short cover letter paragraph.
How many characters is 100 words?
100 words is approximately 600–650 characters including spaces. Character count varies by word length — technical writing with longer words will have more characters per word than casual writing.
How many characters is 250 words?
250 words is approximately 1,500–1,600 characters including spaces. This is a standard short essay length — often the minimum for college applications and scholarship prompts.
How many characters is 300 words?
