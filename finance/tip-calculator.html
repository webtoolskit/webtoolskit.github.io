Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard tip in India?
Tipping in India is voluntary. Around 10% is common at sit-down restaurants when you're happy with the service, and many people simply round up at casual places.
Should I tip on the pre-tax or post-tax amount?
It is generally accepted to tip on the pre-tax amount, though tipping on the total is also fine and appreciated.
What if someone ordered more expensive items?
For fairness, you may want to split based on individual orders. This calculator handles equal splits — for unequal splits, calculate each person's bill separately.
Is a service charge the same as a tip? Is it mandatory in India?
No. Under the Central Consumer Protection Authority's July 2022 guidelines, restaurants and hotels can't add a service charge to your bill automatically or by default, or collect it under another name — any tip is your choice. In March 2025 the Delhi High Court upheld these guidelines. If it's added, you can ask for it to be removed or contact the National Consumer Helpline (1915).
How do I calculate a 10% tip quickly?
Move the decimal point one place to the left. On a ₹1,850 bill, 10% is ₹185. For 5%, halve that (₹92.50); for 15%, add the two (₹277.50).
How do I split a bill with a tip between friends?
Enter the bill, the tip percentage and the number of people. The calculator adds the tip and divides the total equally — for example, ₹3,000 with 10% tip split between 4 people is ₹825 each.
Should I tip if a service charge is already on the bill?
In India, a service charge can't be added by default and you can ask for it to be removed — but if you choose to pay it, you don't need to tip on top. Abroad, check the bill: where service is already included, an extra tip is optional.
Can I use this tip calculator in any currency?
Yes. The maths is the same in any currency — enter the bill in rupees, dollars, pounds or dirhams and read the results in the same currency (the ₹ sign is just a label).
How much should I tip in the USA?
In US restaurants with table service, tips of about 15–20% of the pre-tax bill are customary, because servers' pay relies heavily on tips. Many people tip more for great service.
How much should I tip in the UK or Europe?
In the UK, around 10–12.5% is common at restaurants if service isn't already included — check the bill for a service charge first. In much of Europe, rounding up or leaving 5–10% is typical.
Do people tip in Dubai and the UAE?
Tipping isn't required in the UAE but is appreciated. Around 10% at restaurants is common if service isn't already on the bill, and small tips for delivery riders and hotel staff are customary.
Should I tip food delivery riders?
It's optional but appreciated. Most apps in India let you add a tip in the app, and the full tip is usually passed to the rider — check the app's tipping policy.
💡 Pro Tip: In India, CCPA guidelines (July 2022) say restaurants cannot add a service charge to your bill automatically or by default — any tip is voluntary. If a restaurant insists, you can ask for it to be removed or call the National Consumer Helpline on 1915.
