What is this Petrol Cost Calculator?

This free petrol cost calculator — also called a journey fuel cost calculator or rough petrol cost calculator — helps you find out exactly how much fuel will cost for any trip. Just enter your distance, vehicle mileage and current fuel price to get an instant result.

Whether you're planning a road trip and want to calculate petrol costs in advance, or calculating your daily commute expenses, this tool gives you the accurate numbers. You can also use it to calculate petrol money to split fairly among passengers on a shared trip.

The formula is simple: Fuel Cost = (Distance ÷ Mileage) × Fuel Price. Our calculator does this instantly, with round trip support and monthly cost estimation built in.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate petrol costs for a journey?
The formula to calculate petrol costs is: Fuel Cost = (Distance in km ÷ Vehicle mileage in km/l) × Fuel price per litre. For example, a 100 km journey in a car with 15 km/l mileage at ₹103/litre costs: (100 ÷ 15) × 103 = ₹686.67. This petrol cost calculator does this instantly for any distance.
How much petrol for 13 km?
For a 13 km journey, the petrol needed depends on your vehicle's mileage. At 15 km/l (typical city car): 13 ÷ 15 = 0.87 litres. At ₹103/litre that's about ₹89.50. At 20 km/l (fuel-efficient car): 13 ÷ 20 = 0.65 litres = ₹67. Use the calculator above with your specific mileage for an exact result.
How to work out how much petrol will cost for a journey?
To work out petrol cost for any journey: (1) Find the distance using Google Maps. (2) Note your car's mileage from the manual or fuel fill-up history. (3) Check current petrol price in your city. (4) Use the formula: Cost = (Distance ÷ Mileage) × Price. Or simply enter these three numbers above for an instant result.
What is a rough petrol cost calculator?
A rough petrol cost calculator gives you a quick estimate of fuel costs without needing exact figures. For a rough estimate, use 15 km/l as average mileage for petrol cars and the approximate fuel price in your state. This gives you a ballpark figure for trip planning — useful when you don't know your exact mileage.
Are petrol prices the same across India?
No — petrol and diesel prices vary significantly by state due to different state VAT and cess rates. Metro cities like Mumbai often have higher prices than smaller cities in the same state. Always check the current price at your local petrol pump or the Indian Oil website before planning a long trip.
How do I calculate petrol money to split among passengers?
Calculate the total petrol cost using this calculator, then divide by the number of passengers (including the driver if splitting equally). For example, ₹1,200 total fuel cost among 4 people = ₹300 each. Some people exclude the driver from splitting — adjust based on your arrangement.
How can I improve my car's fuel efficiency?
Maintain correct tyre pressure (under-inflated tyres reduce mileage by 3–5%), service the engine regularly, avoid aggressive acceleration and braking, use cruise control on highways, avoid idling for long periods, and remove unnecessary weight from the boot.
