What is this Tool?

The Water Intake Calculator helps you determine your optimal daily water requirement based on your body weight, physical activity level, and the climate you live in. Water is essential for virtually every bodily function — from regulating body temperature and transporting nutrients to flushing out toxins and maintaining organ function. Most people underestimate how much water they actually need.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does coffee and tea count towards water intake?
A: Partially. While they do contribute to hydration, caffeine has a mild diuretic effect. Count caffeinated beverages at about 80% of their volume towards your water intake.
Q: Is it possible to drink too much water?
A: Yes, though rare. Overhydration (hyponatremia) can be dangerous. Unless you're an endurance athlete, drinking more than 4-5 litres per day without medical supervision is excessive.
Q: Should I drink water before or after meals?
A: Drinking water 30 minutes before meals can aid digestion and help with portion control. Drinking large amounts during meals may dilute digestive enzymes — sip slowly instead.
Q: Why do I still feel thirsty even after drinking water?
A: Excessive thirst can be a sign of diabetes, dehydration from heat, or other conditions. If persistent, consult a doctor.
💡 Pro Tip: Don't wait until you feel thirsty — thirst is already a sign of mild dehydration. Keep a 1-litre water bottle on your desk and aim to refill it at least twice during your workday.
