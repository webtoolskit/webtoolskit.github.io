What is this Tool?

The Body Fat Calculator estimates your body fat percentage using the US Navy circumference method — one of the most accessible and reasonably accurate methods that requires only a measuring tape. Body fat percentage is a more meaningful health indicator than weight or BMI alone, as it distinguishes between fat mass and lean mass (muscle, bone, organs, water).

How to Use This Calculator

1
Select your gender — the formula differs for males and females.
2

Body Fat & Visceral Fat — Common Questions

What is a visceral fat calculator?
Visceral fat is the fat stored deep in the belly around organs such as the liver and intestines. No calculator can measure it from a tape measure — that needs a CT or MRI scan (DEXA can estimate it). What you can check at home is your waist size and waist-to-height ratio, which are good practical signs of risk. This calculator shows both, alongside your total body fat % from the Navy formula.
How do I calculate visceral fat percentage?
You can't get an accurate visceral fat percentage at home. Scans measure it directly, and body composition scales give a visceral fat rating (an index, not a percentage). For a simple check, measure your waist: under Indian guidelines, a waist of 90 cm or more in men or 80 cm or more in women signals higher abdominal fat risk.
What waist size means high belly fat for Indians?
Indian consensus guidelines use a waist of 90 cm or more for men and 80 cm or more for women as a sign of abdominal obesity and higher risk of diabetes and heart disease. These cut-offs are lower than those used in Western countries because South Asians tend to store more fat around the abdomen.
What is waist-to-height ratio?
Waist-to-height ratio is your waist size divided by your height, in the same units. A ratio of 0.5 or more — a waist more than half your height — is linked to higher health risk. At 170 cm tall, that means a waist of 85 cm or more. The calculator shows your ratio automatically.
What is a healthy visceral fat level on a body composition scale?
Brands use different scales, so compare only with your own device. On Omron monitors, 1–9 is normal, 10–14 high and 15–30 very high. On Tanita scales, 1–12 is healthy and 13–59 indicates excess visceral fat. These are ratings, not percentages.
How do I reduce visceral fat?
Visceral fat usually responds well to overall fat loss: a moderate calorie deficit, regular physical activity (both aerobic exercise and strength training help), fewer sugary drinks and refined carbs, less alcohol and enough sleep. You can't target belly fat with specific exercises, but regular exercise tends to reduce visceral fat even when the scale moves slowly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate body fat by waist and neck measurement?
This calculator uses the US Navy formula. For men: body fat % = 495 ÷ (1.0324 − 0.19077 × log10(waist − neck) + 0.15456 × log10(height)) − 450. For women, hips are added: 495 ÷ (1.29579 − 0.35004 × log10(waist + hip − neck) + 0.22100 × log10(height)) − 450. All measurements are in centimetres; the calculator does the maths for you.
Where do I measure my waist, neck and hips?
Neck: just below the Adam's apple, with the tape sloping slightly down at the front. Waist: men at navel level, women at the narrowest part of the waist. Hips (women): at the widest part of the buttocks. Stand relaxed, keep the tape level and snug without pressing into the skin, and measure after a normal breath out.
What is a healthy body fat percentage?
The American Council on Exercise (ACE) categories are widely used. Men: essential fat 2–5%, athletes 6–13%, fitness 14–17%, average 18–24%, obese 25% and above. Women: essential fat 10–13%, athletes 14–20%, fitness 21–24%, average 25–31%, obese 32% and above. The calculator places your result in these categories.
Is the Navy body fat method accurate?
It's a reasonable estimate for tracking progress. Compared with lab methods like DEXA, it's often within about 3–4 percentage points, but it can be further off for very lean or very muscular people and for those with an unusual fat distribution. Measuring carefully and the same way each time matters most.
Why does the calculator ask for my weight?
Weight isn't used in the Navy formula itself. It's used to turn your body fat % into kilograms of fat mass and lean mass (everything else — muscle, bone, organs and water), which is useful for tracking whether you're losing fat or muscle.
Is body fat percentage better than BMI?
They measure different things. BMI only compares weight with height, so muscular people can show a high BMI. Body fat % and waist size tell you more about body composition and belly fat. Using BMI, waist size and body fat % together gives the best picture.
Why do South Asians have higher body fat at the same BMI?
Research shows that South Asians, including Indians, tend to have a higher body fat percentage and more abdominal fat at the same BMI than people of European descent. That's why Indian guidelines use lower BMI and waist cut-offs.
Does body fat percentage change with age?
Body fat usually rises and muscle mass falls with age, especially without strength training. The Navy formula doesn't use age, so the same measurements give the same result at any age — watch the trend in your own numbers over time.
How often should I measure my body fat?
Every 2–4 weeks is enough. Measure at the same time of day (morning before eating is best), with the same tape and technique. Daily measurements mostly show noise from water and food.
Can I reduce body fat without losing weight?
Yes — this is called body recomposition. With strength training and enough protein, you can gain muscle and lose fat at the same time, so your weight may stay similar while your body fat % and waist size go down.
💡 Pro Tip: Visceral fat (fat around internal organs) is more dangerous than subcutaneous fat (fat under the skin). Even people with 'normal' body fat can have high visceral fat. A waist circumference above 90cm for men or 80cm for women in India indicates high visceral fat risk.
