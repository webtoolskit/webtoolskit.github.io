What is this Tool?

The Age Calculator computes your exact age down to years, months, days, hours, and even minutes. Beyond just your age, it tells you the day of the week you were born on, how many days until your next birthday, and expresses your age in alternative units like total days and hours lived. It's fun, informative, and useful for everything from filling forms correctly to planning birthday celebrations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my age in days seem so large?
Because a year has 365 days (366 in a leap year), the total adds up fast — someone who is 30 has lived about 10,957 days. The calculator counts leap days exactly.
How does the calculator handle leap years?
The calculator accounts for leap years when computing total days. Leap years occur every 4 years (except century years not divisible by 400), adding an extra day in February.
Can I calculate someone's age at a past date?
Yes! Change the 'Calculate As Of' date to any past or future date to calculate age at that specific point in time. This is useful for historical research or planning future events.
Is age calculated differently in some countries?
Most countries count age from your date of birth, adding a year on each birthday. South Korea traditionally used "Korean age" (1 at birth, plus a year every New Year), but officially switched to international age for legal and administrative purposes in June 2023.
How do I calculate my exact age?
Enter your date of birth and the date you want to calculate to (today by default). The calculator shows your age in years, months and days, plus total months, days and hours, the day you were born and days until your next birthday.
How do I calculate age as on a specific date for an exam or job form?
Many forms ask for your age "as on" a cut-off date, such as 1 January or 1 August. Set the "Calculate As Of" date to that cut-off and the calculator shows your exact age on that day.
What day of the week was I born?
Enter your date of birth — the result shows the weekday you were born on.
How many days until my next birthday?
The calculator shows the exact number of days left until your next birthday, counting from the "Calculate As Of" date.
How is age calculated for someone born on 29 February?
The calculator uses your real date of birth and counts leap days exactly. In non-leap years, most people celebrate on 28 February or 1 March; for legal purposes, check the rule in your country.
How do I calculate my age in months?
The result shows your total age in months as well as years, months and days — useful for baby milestones and forms that ask for age in months.
How accurate is this age calculator?
It counts the actual calendar days between the two dates, including leap years, so the years, months and days are exact. Hours are based on whole days.
Can I calculate the age difference between two people?
Yes — enter the older person's date of birth as the birth date and the younger person's date of birth as the "Calculate As Of" date. The result is the exact gap between them.
