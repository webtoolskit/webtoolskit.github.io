What is this Tool?

The Ideal Weight Calculator estimates your healthy target weight based on height, gender, and body frame size using established medical formulas. Unlike BMI which gives a single number, ideal weight calculations provide a target range, making them more useful for setting realistic health goals. This tool uses multiple formulas — Hamwi and Devine — for a comprehensive estimate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Are ideal weight formulas accurate for everyone?
A: These formulas were developed for Western populations and may not perfectly apply to all body types. They are best used as general guidelines alongside BMI and body fat percentage.
Q: What is the Hamwi formula?
A: The Hamwi formula calculates ideal weight as 48kg for 5ft height in males (45.4kg for females), adding 2.7kg (2.3kg for females) for each inch above 5ft. Adapted here for metric.
Q: Should I aim for the exact ideal weight?
A: Not necessarily. A range of ±5kg around the ideal weight is considered healthy. Focus on overall fitness, strength, and health markers rather than hitting an exact number on the scale.
Q: Is muscle weight accounted for?
A: No. These formulas don't distinguish between muscle and fat. A very muscular person may weigh more than the 'ideal' and still be extremely healthy. Body composition matters more than weight alone.
💡 Pro Tip: Weight alone is a poor indicator of health. Two people can weigh the same but have very different body compositions — one mostly muscle, one mostly fat. Focus on reducing body fat percentage rather than just the number on the scale.
