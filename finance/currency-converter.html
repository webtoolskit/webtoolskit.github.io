Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Are these exchange rates live?
A: These rates are indicative reference rates for estimation purposes. Always check with your bank or forex provider for the exact rates applicable to your transaction.
Q: Why is the bank rate different?
A: Banks add a margin (typically 1-3%) over the interbank rate. Money transfer services like Wise offer rates closer to the real exchange rate.
Q: What is the best way to convert currency?
A: For travel, use a zero-markup forex card. For transfers, use Wise or Remitly. Avoid airport currency counters as they offer the worst rates.
Q: What is INR?
A: INR stands for Indian Rupee, the official currency of India, denoted by the symbol ₹.
💡 Pro Tip: Avoid converting currency at airports — they typically charge 5-8% above the market rate. Instead, use a multi-currency forex card loaded in India before you travel.
