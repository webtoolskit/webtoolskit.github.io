What is this Tool?

The Scholarship Eligibility Checker helps students discover which scholarships they may qualify for based on their academic performance, family income, category, course level, and state. India has hundreds of government and private scholarships for meritorious and economically disadvantaged students — but many eligible students never apply simply because they don't know they qualify. This tool is a starting point to identify potential opportunities.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What documents are typically needed for scholarships?
A: Common documents include: income certificate from tehsildar/SDM, caste certificate (if applicable), previous year mark sheets, admission letter, bank account details, Aadhaar card, and passport-size photographs.
Q: Where do I apply for government scholarships?
A: National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in) is the central platform for all central government scholarships. State scholarships are available on respective state scholarship portals.
Q: Can I apply for multiple scholarships?
A: Yes, you can apply for multiple scholarships simultaneously. However, some scholarships have conditions that prevent combining with others. Read each scholarship's terms carefully.
Q: What if I am rejected for a scholarship?
A: Common rejection reasons include: incomplete documentation, income certificate not from the right authority, and missing the application deadline. Reapply next year with complete documents, and explore private scholarships from companies and trusts as alternatives.
💡 Pro Tip: Apply for every scholarship you're eligible for, even if you think your chances are low. Many scholarships go unclaimed because eligible students don't apply. The NSP portal (scholarships.gov.in) is the most comprehensive starting point for government scholarships in India.
