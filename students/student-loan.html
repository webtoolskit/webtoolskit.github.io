What is this Education Loan EMI Calculator?

This free education loan EMI calculator helps students and parents calculate the exact monthly EMI for education loans from Credila, SBI, HDFC, Axis Bank, Canara Bank and all major Indian lenders. Simply select your lender from the quick-select buttons — the interest rate fills in automatically — then enter your loan amount and tenure for an instant result.

Unlike a standard EMI calculator, this tool accounts for the moratorium period — the time during which no EMI is paid (typically the course duration + 6 months). Interest accrues during this period, increasing the effective loan amount before repayment begins. This gives you the true, accurate EMI — not an underestimate.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate SBI education loan EMI?
Click the SBI button above — the rate fills in at ~8.65% automatically. Enter your loan amount, course duration, moratorium period and repayment tenure. SBI offers education loans from ₹10 lakh (without collateral) to ₹1.5 crore (with collateral) at rates starting 8.65% — the lowest among major Indian lenders.
What is Navient student loan calculator?
Navient is a US-based student loan servicer. This calculator works for Navient loans — enter your loan amount, interest rate (typically 4–7% for US federal loans) and repayment tenure. Use the Custom Rate option and enter your Navient interest rate for an accurate monthly payment calculation.
What is Nedbank student loan calculator?
Nedbank is a South African bank offering student loans. Use the Custom Rate option in this calculator, enter your Nedbank interest rate and loan details to calculate your monthly repayment. Nedbank student loan rates typically range from 11–15% per annum.
How do I calculate my Credila education loan EMI?
To calculate your Credila education loan EMI: click the Credila button above (sets the rate to ~10.5%), enter your loan amount, course duration, moratorium period (course + 6 months), and repayment tenure (up to 15 years). The calculator accounts for interest accrual during the moratorium period — giving you the accurate post-moratorium EMI. Credila loans typically range from ₹2 lakh to several crores depending on eligibility.
What is Credila education loan interest rate?
Credila (formerly HDFC Credila) offers education loans at interest rates typically ranging from 10.5% to 13% per annum, depending on the course, institution, loan amount and borrower profile. Credila is an NBFC (non-banking financial company) so its rates are generally higher than government banks like SBI (8.65%) but it offers more flexibility — no upper loan limit, multi-city co-borrowers and collateral flexibility.
What is the difference between Credila and SBI education loan?
SBI education loans offer lower interest rates (8.65–10.5%) and are backed by a government bank, making them the first choice for premier institutes. Credila offers higher rates (10.5–13%) but has no upper loan limit, faster processing (21 days), more flexible collateral requirements and can fund institutions that government banks may not cover. For large loans or less conventional courses, Credila is often a better fit.
What is a moratorium period in an education loan?
The moratorium period is the time during which you don't make EMI payments — typically the course duration plus 6–12 months after completion. Interest still accrues during this period, increasing the effective outstanding amount. This calculator accounts for moratorium interest accrual to give you the accurate EMI — unlike simple EMI calculators that ignore this.
Is education loan interest tax deductible?
Yes. Under Section 80E of the Income Tax Act, the entire interest paid on an education loan is deductible from taxable income for up to 8 years from the year repayment begins. This applies to loans from banks and approved financial institutions including Credila. The principal repayment is not deductible — only the interest portion. This benefit can significantly reduce your effective loan cost.
How much education loan can I get without collateral?
Government banks typically offer up to ₹7.5 lakh without collateral. With collateral, amounts up to ₹1.5 crore are available. Credila and private NBFCs can offer higher collateral-free amounts depending on the institution and course — IIT/IIM students can often get ₹20–40 lakh without collateral. The eligible amount depends on the course, institution ranking and co-borrower's income.
What is the student loan planner tool?
This student loan planner calculates your education loan EMI and shows the total repayment amount, total interest paid, and what percentage of your expected starting salary the EMI will consume. A safe rule of thumb is that your EMI should not exceed 25–30% of your monthly starting salary to avoid financial stress in your early career.
💡 Pro Tip: A safe guideline — your total education loan should not exceed 1x your expected annual starting salary. For a ₹10 lakh loan, aim for a job paying at least ₹10 lakh per year (₹83,000/month). This way you can realistically repay the loan within 3–5 years without it becoming a financial burden.
