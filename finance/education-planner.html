What is this Education Cost Planner?

This free education cost planner helps students and parents calculate the total cost of a college degree or professional course in India — including tuition fees, hostel, food, books, transport, laptop and living expenses. Unlike just looking at the fee structure, this tool gives you the real total cost of education including all hidden expenses.

Whether you're planning for B.Tech engineering fees, MBBS costs, MBA expenses or any other course, this planner helps you budget for the full duration and decide whether to take a student loan — and for how much.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the total cost of B.Tech in India?
The total cost of a B.Tech degree in India ranges from ₹1–4 lakh at government colleges to ₹4–20 lakh at private colleges for tuition alone. Adding hostel, food, books and living expenses of approximately ₹1–1.5 lakh per year, the total 4-year cost at a private engineering college is typically ₹8–26 lakh. Use this calculator to plan your specific college's costs.
How much does an MBA cost in India?
MBA fees in India range from ₹15–25 lakh at IIMs to ₹8–20 lakh at top private B-schools and ₹3–8 lakh at smaller private institutes. Living expenses for a 2-year MBA add approximately ₹2–4 lakh. Many students take education loans for MBA given the high expected ROI.
Should I take an education loan for college?
Education loans make sense when: (1) the course has strong career ROI — engineering, MBA, medical; (2) the loan amount is under 10x your expected first-year salary; (3) you've exhausted scholarship options. For a ₹10 lakh loan at 10% over 7 years, the EMI is approximately ₹16,600/month. Use the EMI calculator on this site to plan your repayment.
What is a future college cost calculator?
A future college cost calculator estimates the total cost of a degree or course including tuition fees, hostel, food, books, transport and living expenses — not just the fee structure. This free education cost planner covers all major Indian courses including B.Tech, MBBS, MBA, B.Com and CA, with realistic cost estimates for government and private colleges.
How do I calculate masters degree fees in India?
Select M.Tech or MBA from the course dropdown above — realistic annual fee defaults load automatically. Add your hostel, food and living expenses to get the total 2-year masters cost. M.Tech at government colleges costs ₹1–4 lakh total while MBA at IIMs costs ₹15–25 lakh. Adjust the figures for your specific institution.
What is an online tuition calculator?
An online tuition calculator estimates the total tuition fees for a course over its full duration. Enter your annual tuition fee and course duration above — the planner calculates total tuition cost, total living expenses and overall course cost including all categories. Use it to decide whether to take an education loan and for how much.
How much does education cost in India per year?
Annual education costs in India vary widely: Government engineering colleges ₹25,000–1 lakh/year, Private engineering colleges ₹1–5 lakh/year, MBBS government colleges ₹10,000–50,000/year, Private MBBS ₹5–15 lakh/year, IIM MBA ₹7.5–12.5 lakh/year. Add ₹1–1.5 lakh/year for living expenses for outstation students.
What are the hidden costs of college education?
Hidden costs of college that parents often miss: exam fees (₹2,000–10,000/year), college event fees, uniform/lab coats, project materials, study tours and field trips, sports fees, identity card fees, laptop (₹30,000–80,000 one-time), and internet/mobile recharge costs. This planner includes miscellaneous expenses to account for these.
💡 Pro Tip: Always apply for government scholarships before taking an education loan. National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in) lists hundreds of central and state government scholarships. Even ₹20,000–50,000/year in scholarship reduces your total loan burden significantly over a 4-year course.
