What is a BMI Calculator?

A BMI calculator — or Body Mass Index calculator — measures your body weight relative to your height to determine whether you fall in a healthy weight range. This free BMI checker for men and women uses the standard formula: BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height (m)².

BMI is one of the most widely used health screening tools by doctors worldwide. While it doesn't directly measure body fat, it provides a reliable first-step indicator of weight-related health risks for most adults.

For Indian and South Asian adults, health organisations recommend using slightly lower BMI cutoffs — overweight starts at BMI 23 (not 25) and obese at 27.5 (not 30). This calculator uses Asian-specific cutoffs for more accurate results.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a normal BMI for a 5.8 male?
For a male who is 5'8" (173 cm), a normal BMI is 18.5–22.9 (Asian standards) or 18.5–24.9 (global standards). At 173 cm, this translates to a healthy weight range of approximately 55–69 kg. A BMI of 24.41 for a 5.8 male is in the healthy range by global standards, though slightly above the recommended 22.9 cutoff for South Asians. Use the calculator above with your exact height in cm for a precise result.
Is this BMI calculator for men or women?
This BMI calculator works for both men and women. Select your gender using the dropdown — the calculator shows your BMI category, healthy weight range and ideal weight adjusted for your gender. While the BMI formula (weight ÷ height²) is the same for both sexes, the health interpretation differs: women naturally carry more body fat than men at the same BMI. The ideal weight reference table above also shows separate ranges for men and women.
What would be my ideal weight?
Your ideal weight depends on your height, gender and frame size — not just your BMI. As a general guide, ideal weight corresponds to a BMI of approximately 21–22. For a 170 cm person, this is around 60–63 kg. Enter your height and weight in the BMI calculator above — it shows your specific ideal weight instantly based on your measurements. For a more detailed ideal weight calculation including age and frame size, use our Ideal Weight Calculator.
What is the normal weight range for Indian adults?
For Indian adults, the healthy weight range is based on BMI 18.5–22.9 (Asian-specific guidelines). For a person of average Indian height (165 cm for women, 170 cm for men): women should ideally weigh 50–62 kg, men 53–66 kg. Indian health guidelines use lower BMI thresholds than global WHO standards because South Asians have higher body fat percentage at the same BMI as Caucasians.
How do I calculate correct weight for my height and age?
To find your correct weight for your height and age: (1) Use this BMI calculator for your BMI and healthy range, (2) Use the height vs weight reference table above for your specific height, (3) Use our Ideal Weight Calculator which accounts for age — healthy weight ranges shift slightly for adults over 50. BMI 18.5–22.9 is the healthy target for most Indian adults regardless of age.
What is a healthy BMI for men?
For Indian men, a healthy BMI is between 18.5 and 22.9. BMI 23–27.4 is considered overweight and BMI 27.5 or above is obese using Asian-specific cutoffs. For Western standards, healthy is 18.5–24.9, overweight is 25–29.9, and obese is 30+.
What is a healthy BMI for women?
For Indian women, a healthy BMI is 18.5–22.9. A BMI of 20–22 is often considered the ideal range for women in terms of health outcomes. Use this free weight calculator for women to find your specific healthy range based on your height.
What is the ideal height weight for Indians?
The ideal height weight relationship for Indians follows BMI 20–22 as the optimal range. At 160 cm, ideal weight is approximately 51–56 kg. At 170 cm, ideal weight is approximately 58–64 kg. Use the height vs weight table above for your specific height.
Is BMI different for men and women?
The BMI formula is the same for both, but the health interpretation differs. Women naturally carry more body fat than men at the same BMI. Athletes may show high BMI without being overweight — which is why waist circumference context matters alongside BMI.
Is BMI the same as a weight to age calculator?
BMI is a weight-to-height ratio, not weight-to-age. Healthy BMI ranges do vary slightly by age — older adults (65+) may be healthier at a slightly higher BMI (23–27). For children, BMI is assessed using age- and gender-specific growth charts.
Is BMI accurate for everyone?
BMI is a useful first-step screening tool but has limitations. It may overestimate fat in muscular people and underestimate fat in older adults who have lost muscle. Combine BMI with waist circumference and a doctor's assessment for a complete picture.
💡 Pro Tip: BMI is a starting point, not the complete picture. Waist circumference is equally important — for Indians, a waist above 90cm (men) or 80cm (women) indicates increased health risk regardless of BMI. Measure both for a fuller picture of your health.
