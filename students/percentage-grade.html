What is this Tool?

The Percentage and Grade Calculator instantly converts your raw marks into percentage, letter grade, and grade point. Whether you use the 10-point university grading system, CBSE grading, or simple percentage-based grading, this tool handles all three and shows your result status instantly. It's useful for students at all levels — from school boards to university exams.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the passing percentage in India?
A: Most Indian universities and boards require a minimum of 35% to pass in each subject and 40-45% aggregate. Professional courses like engineering and medicine often require 45-50% to pass.
Q: How does CBSE grading work?
A: CBSE uses a 9-point scale: A1 (91-100%) = 10, A2 (81-90%) = 9, B1 (71-80%) = 8, B2 (61-70%) = 7, C1 (51-60%) = 6, C2 (41-50%) = 5, D (33-40%) = 4, E1/E2 (below 33%) = Fail.
Q: What is a distinction in Indian universities?
A: A distinction is typically awarded for scoring 75% or above in aggregate. First Class is 60-74%, Second Class is 50-59%, and Pass is 35-49%. This varies by university.
What is B- as a percentage?
A B- grade corresponds to approximately 80–82% in most grading systems. In a 4.0 GPA scale, B- = 2.7 GPA. In percentage terms: A+ = 97–100%, A = 93–96%, A- = 90–92%, B+ = 87–89%, B = 83–86%, B- = 80–82%, C+ = 77–79%, C = 73–76%. Use the grade converter above for your specific institution's grading scale.
What is the pass mark for 200?
For a paper with maximum marks of 200, the pass mark at 35% is 70, at 40% is 80, and at 50% is 100. Most Indian universities require 35–40% to pass. Enter your total marks and passing percentage in the calculator above to find the exact pass mark for any maximum marks.
How do I calculate grade from percentage?
Grade from percentage: 90–100% = A+/O (Outstanding), 80–89% = A (Excellent), 70–79% = B+ (Very Good), 60–69% = B (Good), 50–59% = C (Average), 40–49% = D (Pass), below 40% = F (Fail). Different universities use slightly different cutoffs — check your institution's grading policy.
How do I calculate passing marks percentage?
Passing marks = (Pass percentage ÷ 100) × Maximum marks. For example, if max marks is 100 and pass percentage is 35%, passing marks = 35. If max marks is 75 and pass percentage is 40%, passing marks = 30. Use the calculator above — enter your maximum marks and the pass percentage to get the exact passing marks required.
What is 71/80 as a percentage?
71 out of 80 as a percentage = (71 ÷ 80) × 100 = 88.75%. This is a B+ or A- grade in most grading systems. Use the marks calculator above — enter 71 as marks obtained and 80 as maximum marks for instant percentage and grade.
Q: Can I improve my percentage after getting results?
A: Yes, through revaluation (re-checking of answer sheets) or re-examination (supplementary exams). Many universities allow students to improve scores through back paper exams.
💡 Pro Tip: Always verify your university's exact grading scale — they vary significantly. Some universities use 7-point scales, others use 10-point scales with different cutoffs. Your official grade transcript is the definitive reference.
