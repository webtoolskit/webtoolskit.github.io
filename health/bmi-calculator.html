What is a BMI Calculator?

A BMI calculator — or Body Mass Index calculator — measures your body weight relative to your height to determine whether you fall in a healthy weight range. This free BMI checker for men and women uses the standard formula: BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height (m)².

BMI is one of the most widely used health screening tools by doctors worldwide. While it doesn't directly measure body fat, it provides a reliable first-step indicator of weight-related health risks for most adults.

For Indian and South Asian adults, health organisations recommend using slightly lower BMI cutoffs — overweight starts at BMI 23 (not 25) and obese at 27.5 (not 30). This calculator uses Asian-specific cutoffs for more accurate results.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy BMI for men?
For Indian men, a healthy BMI is between 18.5 and 22.9. BMI 23–27.4 is considered overweight and BMI 27.5 or above is obese using Asian-specific cutoffs. For Western standards, healthy is 18.5–24.9, overweight is 25–29.9, and obese is 30+. This BMI checker for men uses Asian-specific thresholds for more relevant results.
What is a healthy BMI for women?
For Indian women, a healthy BMI is 18.5–22.9. Women naturally have slightly higher body fat than men at the same BMI, so context matters. A BMI of 20–22 is often considered the ideal range for women in terms of health outcomes. Use this free weight calculator for women to find your specific healthy range based on your height.
What is the ideal height weight for Indians?
The ideal height weight relationship for Indians follows BMI 20–22 as the optimal range. For example: at 160 cm, ideal weight is approximately 51–56 kg. At 170 cm, ideal weight is approximately 58–64 kg. At 175 cm, ideal weight is approximately 61–67 kg. Use the height vs weight table above for your specific height.
Is BMI different for men and women?
The BMI formula (weight ÷ height²) is the same for both men and women, but the health interpretation differs slightly. Women naturally carry more body fat than men at the same BMI. Athletes and muscular men may show high BMI without being overweight. This is why age, gender and waist circumference context matters alongside BMI.
Is BMI the same as a weight to age calculator?
BMI is a weight-to-height ratio, not a weight-to-age ratio. However, healthy BMI ranges do vary slightly by age — older adults (65+) may be healthier at a slightly higher BMI (23–27) as some extra weight can protect against bone loss. For children, BMI is assessed using age- and gender-specific growth charts, not adult BMI cutoffs.
Is BMI accurate for everyone?
BMI is a useful first-step screening tool but has limitations. It may overestimate fat in muscular people like athletes and underestimate fat in older adults who have lost muscle. For a complete health picture, combine BMI with waist circumference, body fat percentage and a doctor's assessment.
💡 Pro Tip: BMI is a starting point, not the complete picture. Waist circumference is equally important — for Indians, a waist above 90cm (men) or 80cm (women) indicates increased health risk regardless of BMI. Measure both for a fuller picture of your health.
