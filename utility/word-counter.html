What is this Tool?

The Word and Character Counter is an essential writing tool that tracks words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, and estimated reading time in real time as you type or paste text. Whether you're writing a tweet, crafting an Instagram caption, drafting an email, or checking the length of a blog post, this tool ensures your content fits the platform's requirements perfectly.

How to Use This Tool

1
Type or paste your text into the editor.
2
See word count, character count, sentences, paragraphs, and reading time update in real time.
3
Click a platform button (Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn, SMS) to set a character limit.
4
A visual progress bar shows how close you are to the limit.
5
When you exceed the limit, the bar turns red as a warning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the ideal blog post length for SEO?
A: For SEO, aim for 1,500-2,500 words for informational posts. Longer content (2,500+ words) tends to rank better for competitive keywords. However, quality matters more than length — don't pad content unnecessarily.
Q: How many words per minute is the average reading speed?
A: The average adult reads approximately 200-250 words per minute silently. This calculator uses 200 WPM as the baseline. For audio content, the average speaking pace is 130-150 words per minute.
Q: What is the ideal email length?
A: Research suggests emails between 50-125 words get the highest response rates. Marketing emails perform best at 200-500 words. Long emails (1,000+ words) should use clear headings and bullet points for scannability.
Q: Does Twitter count spaces and punctuation?
A: Yes, every character including spaces, punctuation, and emojis counts toward Twitter's 280-character limit. URLs are automatically shortened to 23 characters regardless of actual length.
💡 Pro Tip: For social media, write your full message first without worrying about the character limit, then edit down. Cutting 20% of your words almost always makes writing stronger and more impactful.
