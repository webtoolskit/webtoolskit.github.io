What is this Tool?

The Wedding Budget Calculator helps couples and families plan a wedding within their means by allocating budget across all major expense categories. Indian weddings are among the most elaborate and expensive celebrations in the world, often involving multiple events over several days. Without proper planning, wedding costs can spiral out of control. This tool helps you set realistic budgets for venue, catering, decoration, photography, attire, jewellery, and other expenses.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the average Indian wedding cost?
A: Indian weddings vary enormously — from ₹5 lakh for a simple ceremony to several crores for elaborate multi-day celebrations. The national average is estimated at ₹20-50 lakh.
Q: Which wedding expenses are the biggest?
A: Venue and catering typically account for 40-50% of the total budget. Jewellery and clothing are the next biggest categories.
Q: How can I reduce wedding costs without compromising on experience?
A: Opt for weekday weddings (venues are cheaper), limit the guest list, choose local seasonal flowers, consider a morning or afternoon ceremony, and book vendors in off-season.
Q: Should I take a personal loan for wedding expenses?
A: Financial advisors generally advise against taking high-interest personal loans for weddings. If needed, limit borrowing to amounts you can comfortably repay within 12-18 months.
💡 Pro Tip: The single most effective way to reduce wedding costs is to reduce the guest list. Catering typically costs ₹800-₹2,500 per person — cutting 100 guests saves ₹1-2.5 lakh instantly.
