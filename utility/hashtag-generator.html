What is this Tool?

The Hashtag Generator creates a relevant set of hashtags for your social media posts based on your topic, keywords, and target platform. The right hashtags help platforms understand what your post is about and help people interested in that topic find it. Instagram now allows up to 5 hashtags per post, so choosing a few precise ones matters more than ever.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many hashtags can I use on Instagram?
Instagram now allows a maximum of 5 hashtags per post or reel, and says a few specific hashtags work better than many generic ones. This generator highlights your top 5 picks for Instagram.
Should hashtags go in the caption or the comments?
The caption is the simplest place, and it keeps your hashtags attached to the post itself. With the 5-hashtag limit, focus on choosing the most relevant ones rather than where they go.
How many hashtags should I use on LinkedIn?
Use a small number — about 3 to 5 — that clearly describe your topic or industry. More than that tends to look cluttered and adds little.
Are some Instagram hashtags restricted?
Yes. Instagram can limit hashtags that are linked to spam or content that breaks its rules. If a hashtag page shows few or no recent posts, avoid it. Rotating different relevant hashtags also looks more natural than repeating the same set on every post.
How does this hashtag generator work?
It turns your keywords into hashtags, recognises common topics like fitness, food, travel, tech, education, business and motivation, and suggests related hashtags from that topic. Your strongest matches are highlighted as top picks for the platform you choose.
How many hashtags should I use on X (Twitter)?
One or two relevant hashtags is usually enough on X. More than that uses up your character limit and can make posts harder to read.
Do hashtags still help reach on Instagram?
They help Instagram understand what your post is about, but Instagram has said hashtags matter less for reach than they used to — the content itself, captions and engagement matter more. Use a few precise hashtags that genuinely describe the post.
Should I use broad or niche hashtags?
Mix both, leaning towards specific ones. A very broad tag like #love is flooded with posts, while a specific one like #homecooking or #travelindia reaches people looking for exactly your topic.
Can I use the same hashtags on every post?
It's better to rotate. Keep a few sets of relevant hashtags for each content theme and choose the set that best fits each post.
Should I create my own branded hashtag?
A short, unique branded hashtag can help your followers find and share your content, especially for a business, event or campaign. Check it isn't already used for something else first.
Are hashtags case-sensitive?
No — #TravelIndia and #travelindia lead to the same place. Capitalising each word (#TravelIndia) makes multi-word hashtags easier to read, including for screen-reader users.
Why didn't I get many hashtag ideas?
The generator suggests related hashtags when it recognises a topic. If your keywords are very specific, add a broader word such as fitness, food, travel, tech, business or study to get more ideas.
