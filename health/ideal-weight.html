What is an Age to Weight Calculator?

An age to weight calculator — also called an ideal weight by age calculator or weight calculator for age — helps you find your healthy weight range based on your height, age and gender. Unlike a simple BMI calculator, this tool uses four established formulas (Hamwi, Devine, Robinson and Miller) and shows them next to the healthy BMI weight range for your height. Your age doesn't change the formulas — but the calculator tells you if the adult formulas don't apply (under 18) or if your doctor may accept a slightly higher weight (over 65).

This free ideal weight calculator by age and height is useful for anyone wondering "what should I weigh at my age?" — whether you're 20, 35, 45 or 60. These formulas were originally created for clinical uses such as medication dosing, so treat the result as a rough target to discuss with your doctor, not a strict rule.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an age to weight calculator?
People often search for an "age to weight calculator" when they want to know what they should weigh. For adults, healthy weight depends on height and sex, not age — the standard formulas don't change after 18. This calculator uses your age only to add a note if you're under 18 (use growth charts instead) or over 65 (your doctor may accept a slightly higher weight).
Does ideal weight change with age?
The formulas don't change with age. What changes is body composition: most adults lose muscle and gain fat as they get older, so staying active and strong matters more than a number on the scale. Some research suggests a slightly higher BMI may be fine in people over 65 — ask your doctor what's right for you.
What is the Hamwi formula for ideal weight?
Hamwi (1964): men 48 kg for the first 152.4 cm (5 feet) plus about 2.7 kg per extra inch; women 45.4 kg plus about 2.3 kg per extra inch. It's the original "106 lb + 6 lb per inch" rule for men and "100 lb + 5 lb per inch" for women.
What are the Devine, Robinson and Miller formulas?
All use the weight at 5 feet plus a set amount per extra inch. Devine (1974): men 50 kg + 2.3 kg/inch, women 45.5 kg + 2.3 kg/inch. Robinson (1983): men 52 kg + 1.9 kg/inch, women 49 kg + 1.7 kg/inch. Miller (1983): men 56.2 kg + 1.41 kg/inch, women 53.1 kg + 1.36 kg/inch.
Which ideal weight formula is most accurate?
None of them is "correct" for everyone — they were built from different data, and Devine was originally created for medication dosing. That's why the calculator shows the range across all four, plus the healthy BMI range, instead of a single number.
How do I know my frame size?
A quick check is to wrap your thumb and middle finger around your opposite wrist: if they overlap, you likely have a small frame; if they just touch, medium; if they don't meet, large. The calculator adjusts the formulas by about 10% down or up for small or large frames.
What is a healthy weight for a 40-year-old woman?
It depends on height, not age. For a 160 cm woman, the formulas give about 52–57 kg and the healthy BMI range (18.5–22.9) is about 47–59 kg — the same as at 25 or 55. Enter your own height above.
What is the ideal weight for a 23- or 24-year-old?
Age doesn't change the result for adults. A 170 cm man's formula range is about 65–67 kg (healthy BMI range 53.5–66.2 kg), and a 160 cm woman's is about 52–57 kg (47.4–58.6 kg). Enter your own height and gender above for your range.
What is the difference between ideal weight and average weight?
Average weight is simply what people of a certain height typically weigh — and in many countries that's above the healthy range. Ideal weight is a health-based target. This calculator gives health-based targets, not population averages.
Why is my ideal weight different from the BMI range?
The formulas give a narrow target, while the BMI range covers all healthy weights for your height. For short heights the formulas often sit near the top of the BMI range; for tall people they can sit lower. Anywhere within the healthy BMI range is generally considered healthy.

For Men & Women — How the Calculator Differs

Is this ideal weight calculator for men or women?
Both. Select your gender and the calculator switches to the male or female version of each formula. At the same height, men's formula results are usually a few kilograms higher than women's because men generally carry more muscle and bone mass.
Is the healthy BMI range the same for men and women?
Yes. The healthy BMI range — 18.5–22.9 under Indian guidelines, or 18.5–24.9 on the global WHO scale — is the same for adult men and women, so the "Healthy Range" result doesn't change with gender. Only the formula-based ideal weights differ.
How much should I weigh for my height?
Enter your height above. As a quick guide using the Indian healthy BMI range: at 155 cm about 44–55 kg, at 165 cm about 50–62 kg, and at 175 cm about 57–70 kg. The formula results give a narrower target within or near those ranges.
💡 Pro Tip: Weight alone is a poor indicator of health. Two people can weigh exactly the same but have completely different body compositions — one mostly muscle, one mostly fat. Always look at weight alongside body fat percentage and waist circumference for the full picture.
