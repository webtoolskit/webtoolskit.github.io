What is this Tool?

The Notice Period Calculator helps you calculate your exact last working day after submitting your resignation. Whether you're switching jobs, starting freelancing, or taking a career break, knowing your exact last working day helps you plan your transition, negotiate with your new employer, and handle the handover process professionally. The calculator handles both calendar days and working days.

How to Use This Tool

1
Enter the date you plan to submit your resignation letter.
2
Select your notice period from the dropdown or enter a custom number of days.
3
Choose whether your notice period counts weekends (calendar days) or excludes them (working days only).
4
Your last working day and the number of working days remaining are shown instantly.

Why This Matters

  • Plan your joining date at a new company with confidence
  • Calculate notice period buyout amount if leaving early
  • Avoid disputes with your current employer about the last working day
  • Plan handover of responsibilities within the notice period
  • Time your resignation to coincide with bonus payments or vesting dates

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can my employer extend my notice period?
A: Your employment contract defines the notice period. Your employer cannot unilaterally extend it unless you agree. However, they can ask you to serve a longer period by mutual agreement.
Q: What is notice period buyout?
A: Instead of serving the full notice period, you can pay your employer the equivalent salary for the remaining days. This is called notice period buyout. The amount is typically your basic salary for the remaining days.
Q: Can I negotiate a shorter notice period?
A: Yes. If you have a replacement ready, all handovers are complete, or you have a compelling reason, many employers agree to early release. Always get early release in writing.
Q: Should I resign on a Monday or Friday?
A: Resigning on a Friday gives both parties the weekend to process the news. However, resigning on a Monday means you leave on a Monday — giving you the full weekend before starting your new role. Choose based on what suits your new employer's joining date.
💡 Pro Tip: If your new employer wants you to join before your notice period ends, negotiate a joining date that works and request early release from your current employer in writing. Many employers are flexible if the handover is smooth.
