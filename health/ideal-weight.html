What is an Age to Weight Calculator?

An age to weight calculator — also called an ideal weight by age calculator or weight calculator for age — helps you find your healthy weight range based on your height, age and gender. Unlike a simple BMI calculator, this tool uses two established medical formulas (Hamwi and Devine) and adjusts the result slightly for age, since healthy weight ranges shift as we grow older.

This free ideal weight calculator by age and height is useful for anyone wondering "what should I weigh at my age?" — whether you're 20, 35, 45 or 60. The formulas used are the same ones medical professionals use as a starting point for weight management discussions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an age to weight calculator?
An age to weight calculator estimates your ideal healthy weight based on your age, height and gender. It uses medical formulas (Hamwi and Devine) and applies a slight age adjustment — since what's considered a healthy weight range shifts slightly as we get older. Enter your details above for an instant personalised result.
What is the ideal weight for my age and height?
Ideal weight varies by height, gender and frame size — not just age. However, age does affect the range slightly. For example, a 170 cm male aged 25 has an ideal range of approximately 58–72 kg, while the same person at 50 may have a slightly wider healthy range of 62–78 kg. Use the calculator above with your exact measurements for a personalised result.
Does ideal weight change with age?
Yes, slightly. As we age, muscle mass naturally decreases and body composition changes. Most health guidelines allow for a slightly higher weight range for adults over 50, as some extra weight can help protect bone density and reduce the risk of fractures. However, the change is small — the biggest factor is always height, not age.
What is the Hamwi formula for ideal weight?
The Hamwi formula calculates ideal weight as 48 kg for a male of 152.4 cm (5 ft), adding 2.7 kg for every 2.54 cm (1 inch) above that height. For females, it starts at 45.4 kg and adds 2.3 kg per inch. Frame size adjustments of ±2.5 kg are applied for small or large frames.
Is ideal weight different for men and women?
Yes — women's ideal weight is generally 3–5 kg lower than men's at the same height, because women naturally have higher body fat percentage and less muscle mass. Both the Hamwi and Devine formulas use separate calculations for males and females to account for this difference.
What is a healthy weight for a 40-year-old woman?
For a 40-year-old woman of average height (160 cm) with a medium frame, the healthy weight range is approximately 54–66 kg. This is based on the Hamwi and Devine formulas with an age adjustment. Taller women will have a higher healthy weight range — use the calculator above with your exact height for a personalised result.

For Men & Women — How the Calculator Differs

Is this ideal weight calculator for men or women?
This calculator works for both men and women — select your gender using the dropdown and the formula changes automatically. The Hamwi formula uses different starting values for males (48 kg base at 152.4 cm) and females (45.4 kg base at 152.4 cm), and the per-inch increment also differs. Women's ideal weight is typically 3–5 kg lower than men's at the same height due to natural differences in muscle mass and body composition.
Is BMI and ideal weight the same for men and women?
The BMI range (18.5–24.9 for healthy weight) is the same for both men and women, but ideal weight calculations differ by gender. Men naturally carry more muscle mass, so their ideal weight at the same height is higher than women's. This calculator uses gender-specific Hamwi and Devine formulas to give you an accurate ideal weight range based on your gender, height, age and frame size.
💡 Pro Tip: Weight alone is a poor indicator of health. Two people can weigh exactly the same but have completely different body compositions — one mostly muscle, one mostly fat. Always look at weight alongside body fat percentage and waist circumference for the full picture.
