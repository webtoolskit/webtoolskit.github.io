What is this Tool?

The Meta Title and Description Checker helps website owners, bloggers, and SEO professionals ensure their page titles and meta descriptions are the optimal length for Google search results. These two elements are the most visible parts of your page in search results — they directly impact your click-through rate (CTR) and ultimately how much organic traffic your page receives.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What happens if my title is too long?
A: Google truncates titles longer than approximately 60 characters (580 pixels) with an ellipsis (…). The cut-off point can vary based on the specific characters used, as some letters are wider than others.
Q: Does meta description affect ranking?
A: Meta descriptions don't directly affect rankings, but they significantly impact click-through rates. A compelling, relevant description that matches the searcher's intent can dramatically increase organic traffic.
Q: What makes a good meta title?
A: Include your primary keyword near the beginning, keep it under 60 characters, make it descriptive and compelling, and include your brand name at the end if space allows.
Q: Can Google rewrite my meta description?
A: Yes, Google frequently rewrites meta descriptions if it thinks a different snippet from your page better matches the user's search query. This happens in about 62% of searches. Write a great description as a default, but accept that Google may change it.
💡 Pro Tip: Your meta title is your most valuable SEO real estate. Include your primary keyword as close to the beginning as naturally possible — Google gives more weight to words that appear earlier in the title tag.
