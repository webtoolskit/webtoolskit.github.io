Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a free tool that creates personalized NEET study schedules based on weak subjects?
Yes — this is exactly what this NEET Study Planner does. Set your confidence level for Physics, Chemistry and Biology, tap your specific weak chapters, and the tool generates a personalized daily schedule that gives more time to your weakest subjects automatically. Completely free, no signup required.
How many hours should I study for NEET per day?
Most NEET toppers study 8–12 hours per day in the final 3–6 months before the exam. However, 6 focused hours with active recall beats 12 distracted hours every time. Set your realistic daily hours in this planner — it builds your schedule accordingly and includes breaks.
How should I split time between Physics, Chemistry and Biology for NEET?
The recommended split for NEET is: Biology 40%, Chemistry 30%, Physics 30% — since Biology carries 360 out of 720 marks. However, if Physics is your weakest subject, increase Physics time significantly. This planner adjusts the split automatically based on your confidence sliders.
How many mock tests should I take for NEET preparation?
Start with 1 mock per week 6 months before NEET, increase to 2–3 per week in the final 3 months, and give a mock every day in the last 2 weeks. This planner schedules mock tests automatically based on your selected frequency.
Which Biology chapters are most important for NEET?
Human Physiology, Genetics & Evolution, Reproduction, and Ecology consistently carry the highest weightage in NEET Biology. NCERT Biology (Class 11 + 12) covers 90%+ of NEET Biology — read it line by line at least 5 times. Mark any chapters you find difficult using this planner.
How do I recover attendance and still prepare for NEET?
Use the WebToolsKit Attendance Calculator to track your college attendance separately, and this NEET Study Planner for your self-study schedule. Plan your study hours around college timing — even 4 focused hours of self-study daily adds up to 120+ hours per month.
💡 NEET Topper Strategy: The single most effective NEET preparation habit is daily NCERT revision. Read at least one NCERT chapter of Biology every day without fail — even during the final week. Most NEET questions come directly from NCERT sentences. Use this planner to schedule a mandatory daily NCERT revision slot.
