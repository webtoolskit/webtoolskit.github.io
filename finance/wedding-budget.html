What is this Indian Wedding Budget Calculator?

This free Indian wedding budget calculator — also called a marriage cost calculator or wedding budget estimator — helps couples and families plan a wedding by allocating their total budget across all major expense categories. Indian weddings typically involve multiple ceremonies over 2–5 days, making budget planning especially important to avoid overspending.

Whether you're planning a simple ₹5 lakh wedding or an elaborate ₹50 lakh+ celebration, this tool helps you see exactly where your money is going across venue, catering, decor, photography, attire, jewellery, mehendi, music and other costs.

Typical Indian Wedding Budget Allocation

  • Venue & Logistics — 20–25% of total budget (biggest single cost)
  • Catering & Food — 20–30% (₹800–2,500 per guest for a full meal)
  • Jewellery (Bride) — 15–20% (gold + diamond + silver sets)
  • Bridal Attire & Groom Clothing — 8–12% (lehenga, sherwani, accessories)
  • Decoration & Flowers — 8–12% (mandap, entrance, stage)
  • Photography & Videography — 5–8% (pre-wedding + wedding day)
  • Music & Entertainment — 3–5% (DJ, band, sangeet performers)
  • Mehendi & Beauty — 2–4% (bridal mehendi, salon, makeup artist)
  • Invitations & Gifts — 2–3% (cards, return gifts for guests)
  • Honeymoon — 5–10% (domestic or international travel)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average Indian wedding cost?
The average Indian wedding costs between ₹20–35 lakh for a middle-class family with 200–400 guests. Simple weddings start at ₹3–8 lakh, while grand weddings in premium venues can cost ₹50 lakh to several crores. Use this Indian wedding budget calculator to plan based on your specific guest count and priorities.
How do I calculate marriage cost in India?
To calculate marriage cost in India: start with your guest count × catering cost per person (₹800–2,500), add venue cost (₹1–5 lakh for banquet halls, ₹5–20 lakh for premium venues), then add jewellery, attire, decor, photography and other costs. This marriage cost calculator does all this automatically — just set your total budget and adjust the category sliders.
How much does catering cost per person at Indian weddings?
Indian wedding catering costs ₹800–1,200 per person for a simple vegetarian buffet, ₹1,200–2,000 for a standard multi-cuisine spread, and ₹2,000–3,500 per person for a premium spread with live counters and dessert stations. For 300 guests at ₹1,500/person, catering alone costs ₹4.5 lakh.
What is the biggest expense in an Indian wedding?
Jewellery and venue/catering are typically the two biggest expenses in Indian weddings. Jewellery (gold + diamond sets for the bride) can account for 15–25% of the total budget. Venue + catering combined often takes 40–50% of the budget. This is why reducing the guest list is the most effective way to control costs.
How can I reduce Indian wedding costs without compromising experience?
The most effective ways to reduce wedding costs: (1) Reduce guest list — catering at ₹1,500/person means 100 fewer guests = ₹1.5 lakh saved. (2) Choose weekday weddings — venues charge 20–30% less. (3) Opt for morning or afternoon ceremonies. (4) Rent decor instead of buying. (5) Book photographers in off-season (July–September). (6) Consider destination weddings which often work out cheaper for smaller guest lists.
Should I take a personal loan for wedding expenses?
Financial advisors generally advise against taking high-interest personal loans for weddings. If a loan is unavoidable, limit borrowing to amounts repayable within 12–18 months. At 14% interest on a ₹5 lakh personal loan over 2 years, you pay ₹75,000+ in interest alone. Consider reducing the scale of the wedding instead of taking on debt that follows you into married life.
What is a wedding budget estimator?
A wedding budget estimator — like this free tool — helps you allocate your total wedding budget across all major expense categories before you start booking vendors. It gives you a realistic picture of what you can afford in each category, preventing the common mistake of overspending on one area (like decor) and running short for another (like catering).
💡 Pro Tip: The single most effective way to reduce Indian wedding costs is to reduce the guest list. Catering at ₹1,500 per person means every 100 guests you cut saves ₹1.5 lakh — money that could go towards better photography, a nicer venue or your honeymoon.
