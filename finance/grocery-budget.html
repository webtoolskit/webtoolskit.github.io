What is this Grocery Budget Planner?

This free grocery budget planner — also called a grocery spending calculator or food shopping budget planner — helps Indian households plan and track their monthly food expenses by category. With food inflation steadily rising in India, tracking grocery spend by category is one of the most effective ways to control household expenses.

Enter your monthly spend for vegetables, fruits, dairy, grains, meat, snacks, personal care and other categories to get a complete picture of your monthly grocery budget. The tool calculates your total monthly spend, per-person cost, and annual grocery expense — helping you identify where to cut back.

How to Reduce Your Monthly Grocery Bill

  • Buy seasonal vegetables and fruits — they are 40–60% cheaper than off-season produce
  • Shop at local sabzi mandis and kirana stores instead of supermarkets for staples
  • Plan your weekly meals before shopping — reduces impulse buying by 20–30%
  • Buy grains, pulses and rice in bulk (5–10 kg) — saves 10–15% vs small packs
  • Reduce packaged and processed foods — they cost 3–5x more per serving than home-cooked equivalents
  • Use grocery apps like Zepto, Blinkit or BigBasket for weekly deals and offers
  • Reduce food waste — plan portions and store vegetables correctly to extend shelf life

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a grocery spending calculator?
A grocery spending calculator — like this free tool — helps you plan your monthly food and household shopping budget by category. Enter your expected spend for vegetables, dairy, grains, snacks and other categories to get your total monthly grocery budget with per-person and annual breakdowns.
What is a reasonable monthly grocery budget for a family of 4 in India?
A family of 4 in a Tier-1 Indian city typically spends ₹8,000–15,000 per month on groceries, depending on dietary preferences and lifestyle. Vegetarian households tend to spend 20–30% less than non-vegetarian households. In Tier-2 cities, the same family can manage on ₹6,000–10,000 per month.
How do I create a food shopping budget planner?
To create a grocery budget: list all grocery categories (vegetables, fruits, dairy, grains, meat, snacks, personal care), estimate monthly spend for each, add them up for your total monthly grocery budget, then track actual spending weekly. This tool does this automatically — just enter your expected spend per category.
How much should I spend on groceries per person per month in India?
A rough guideline for India: ₹1,500–2,500 per person per month for basic home-cooked vegetarian meals, ₹2,500–4,000 per person for mixed diet with some non-vegetarian and packaged foods. In metro cities, add 20–30% to these estimates. Use the calculator above with your family size for a personalised breakdown.
What percentage of income should go to groceries?
A common budgeting guideline is to keep total food expenses (groceries + eating out) under 15–20% of net monthly income. For groceries alone, aim for 10–15% of income. If you're spending more, use this grocery budget planner to identify which categories are over-budget.
Should I buy groceries in bulk to save money?
For non-perishables like rice, dal, atta, sugar and oils — yes, buying in 5–10 kg quantities saves 10–15%. For vegetables and fruits — no, buying perishables in bulk leads to wastage which ends up more expensive. The key is bulk buying only items you use regularly and can store safely.
💡 Pro Tip: Plan your week's meals before grocery shopping and make a strict list. Shoppers with a grocery list spend 23% less than those who shop without one. Use this planner every month — just 10 minutes of planning saves ₹500–1,500 per month on average.
