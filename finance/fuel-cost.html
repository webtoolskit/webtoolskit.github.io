What is this Fuel Cost Calculator?

This free fuel cost calculator works for both metric (km/litres) and imperial (miles/MPG) systems — making it useful for drivers in India, the UK, and the US. Whether you want to calculate petrol cost for 44 miles, plan a road trip in kilometres, or work out your daily commute cost, this tool gives you an instant result.

Switch between km/Litres mode for Indian drivers and Miles/MPG mode for UK and US drivers using the toggle at the top of the calculator. The formula is: Fuel Cost = (Distance ÷ Efficiency) × Fuel Price — automatically adjusted for your chosen unit system.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate petrol cost for 44 miles?
To calculate petrol cost for 44 miles: switch to Miles/MPG mode in the calculator above, enter 44 as the distance, enter your car's MPG, and enter the petrol price per litre. The calculator converts miles to km (44 miles = 70.8 km), calculates litres needed, and gives you the exact cost instantly. For a typical UK car at 40 MPG with petrol at £1.50/litre, 44 miles costs approximately £7.50.
How do I calculate petrol cost per mile?
To calculate petrol cost per mile: divide your car's fuel consumption (litres per mile) by your MPG, then multiply by the petrol price per litre. For example, at 40 MPG and £1.50/litre: 1 ÷ 40 × 4.546 × 1.50 = £0.17 per mile. The calculator shows cost per mile automatically in Miles/MPG mode.
How much petrol does a 100 mile journey use?
A 100 mile journey uses approximately 2.27 gallons (10.3 litres) in a car getting 44 MPG, or 3.33 gallons (15.1 litres) in a car getting 30 MPG. Enter your exact distance and MPG in the Miles/MPG mode above for a precise result.
How do I calculate petrol costs for a journey in km?
Use km/Litres mode: Fuel Cost = (Distance in km ÷ Vehicle mileage in km/l) × Fuel price per litre. For example, 100 km at 15 km/l at ₹103/litre = ₹686.67. This is the standard formula used for Indian vehicles.
What is the difference between MPG and km/l?
MPG (miles per gallon) is used in the UK and US, while km/l (kilometres per litre) is used in India and most of Asia. To convert: multiply km/l by 2.825 to get UK MPG, or multiply km/l by 2.352 to get US MPG. For example, 15 km/l = 42.4 UK MPG.
How do I split petrol cost among passengers?
Calculate the total petrol cost using this calculator, then divide by the number of passengers. For example, £15 total fuel cost among 3 people = £5 each. Round trip mode doubles the distance automatically for return journey splits.
