Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check if my password is strong?
Type your password in the checker above — it instantly scores it out of 100 and shows exactly which requirements it meets or fails. A strong password scores 75+ and includes 12+ characters, uppercase, lowercase, numbers and special characters. The tool also shows how long it would take a hacker to crack it using brute force.
How safe is my password?
Your password's safety depends on its length and complexity. An 8-character password with mixed characters can be cracked in hours. A 12-character password takes years. A 16+ character password with symbols takes centuries. Use the crack time estimate above to see exactly how safe your password is.
How secure is my password?
This strong password checker tests your password against 8 security rules — length, uppercase, lowercase, numbers, special characters and common patterns. A score of 90–100 means your password is very secure. Below 50 means it needs improvement. The tool runs entirely in your browser — your password is never sent to any server.
What is a strong password checker?
A strong password checker is a tool that analyses your password's strength in real time and tells you if it's weak, fair, strong or very strong. This free online password strength checker tests length, character variety, patterns and estimates crack time — all without sending your password anywhere.
How hackable is my password?
Your password's hackability depends on its entropy — the number of possible combinations. Short passwords under 8 characters are crackable instantly. Passwords with only lowercase letters are far weaker than those mixing cases, numbers and symbols. This tool estimates crack time based on 1 trillion guesses per second — the speed of modern GPU-based cracking tools.
Q: What makes a password strong?
A: Length (16+ characters), a mix of uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters (!@#$%^&*), avoiding dictionary words, and not using personal information like your name or birthday.
Q: Should I use a password manager?
A: Absolutely yes. Password managers like Bitwarden (free), 1Password, or Dashlane generate and store unique, strong passwords for every site. You only need to remember one master password. This is the single best thing you can do for your online security.
Q: Is it safe to type my password in this tool?
A: Yes. This tool runs entirely in your browser. Your password is never transmitted to any server. You can verify this by disconnecting from the internet and testing — the tool still works perfectly.
Q: What is a passphrase?
A: A passphrase is a string of random words (e.g., 'correct horse battery staple') that is easier to remember than a random string of characters but equally or more secure due to its length. 4-5 random words create an extremely strong password.
💡 Pro Tip: The #1 rule of password security: never reuse passwords across multiple sites. If one site gets hacked and your password is exposed, attackers will immediately try that same password on every major website. Use a unique password for every account.
