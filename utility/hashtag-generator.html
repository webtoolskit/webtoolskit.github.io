What is this Tool?

The Hashtag Generator creates a relevant set of hashtags for your social media posts based on your topic, keywords, and target platform. Using the right hashtags dramatically increases the organic reach of your social media content — they act as a discovery mechanism that connects your posts with people who don't yet follow you but are interested in your topic.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How many hashtags should I use on Instagram?
A: Instagram allows up to 30 hashtags. Research suggests 9-15 hashtags per post is the optimal range for reach and engagement. Using all 30 can look spammy and may reduce reach.
Q: Should I put hashtags in the caption or comments?
A: Both work equally for reach. Many creators prefer putting hashtags in the first comment to keep captions clean and readable. Instagram has confirmed both approaches are treated the same by the algorithm.
Q: Do hashtags work on LinkedIn?
A: Yes, but use fewer — 3-5 highly relevant hashtags. LinkedIn's algorithm treats hashtag-heavy posts as lower quality. Choose hashtags with active communities (100K-5M followers) for best reach.
Q: Are banned hashtags a thing?
A: Yes. Instagram occasionally restricts certain hashtags due to abuse. If a hashtag shows no recent posts when you search it, it may be shadow-banned. Avoid using the same exact hashtag set on every post, as this can trigger spam filters.
💡 Pro Tip: Build a hashtag library for your top 5-10 content themes and rotate between sets. Never use the exact same 15 hashtags on every post — Instagram's algorithm may classify this as repetitive spammy behaviour and reduce your reach.
