What is this Tool?

The Word and Character Counter is an essential writing tool that tracks words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, and estimated reading time in real time as you type or paste text. Whether you're writing a tweet, crafting an Instagram caption, drafting an email, or checking the length of a blog post, this tool ensures your content fits the platform's requirements perfectly.

How to Use This Tool

1
Type or paste your text into the editor.
2
See word count, character count, sentences, paragraphs, and reading time update in real time.
3
Click a platform button (Twitter, Threads, Instagram, LinkedIn, SMS) to set a character limit.
4
A visual progress bar shows how close you are to the limit.
5
When you exceed the limit, the bar turns red as a warning.

  • Write within platform character limits with confidence
  • Track blog post length for optimal SEO performance
  • Ensure SMS messages don't exceed the 160-character limit

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a live word count tool?
A live word counter counts your words, characters, sentences and paragraphs as you type, without clicking a button. Paste or type your text above and every count updates instantly, along with the estimated reading time.
How do I check the length of my text?
Paste or type it into the box above to see character count, word count, sentences, paragraphs and reading time at once. Use the platform buttons (X, Threads, Instagram, LinkedIn, SMS) to check your text against their character limits.
How many characters is one word on average?
In English, an average word is about 4.7 letters, so with the space after it a word takes roughly 5.5–6.5 characters. That's the rule used for the estimates on this page — your exact count depends on your writing, so paste it above to check.
How many words is 140 characters?
About 20–25 words. 140 characters was the original Twitter limit — enough for one or two short sentences.
How many words is 255 characters?
About 40–45 words, or three to four short sentences. 255 characters is a common limit for database text fields and some form inputs.
How many words is 280 characters?
About 45–50 words. 280 characters is the standard post limit on X (Twitter) for non-premium accounts.
How many words is 300 characters?
About 45–55 words, or three to four sentences — a typical length for a short product description or push notification.
How many words is 500 characters?
About 75–90 words. 500 characters is the post limit on Threads.
How many words is 750 characters?
About 115–135 words — roughly one solid paragraph, or six to eight sentences.
How many words is 1,000 characters?
About 155–180 words. Many application forms and review sites use a 1,000-character limit.
How many words is 2,000 characters?
About 300–360 words, or two to three paragraphs.
How many words is 2,300 characters?
About 355–420 words, or three to four paragraphs.
How many words is 2,500 characters?
About 385–455 words — roughly a one-page letter or a short article section.
How many words is 8,000 characters?
About 1,230–1,450 words, which takes around 5–7 minutes to read at 200–250 words per minute.
How many words is 9,000 characters?
About 1,380–1,640 words, or roughly a 6–8 minute read.
How many characters is 100 words?
About 550–650 characters including spaces.
How many characters is 250 words?
About 1,400–1,600 characters including spaces — a common length for short essays and application answers.
How many characters is 300 words?
About 1,650–1,950 characters including spaces.
How many sentences is 140 characters?
Usually one or two short sentences, since an average sentence of 15–20 words runs about 90–130 characters.
How many sentences is 280 characters?
Usually two or three sentences.
How many sentences is 750 characters?
About six to eight sentences at an average length of 15–20 words each.
How many sentences is 1,500 characters?
About 12–16 sentences — roughly two paragraphs.
What is the Threads character limit?
Threads allows up to 500 characters per post, which is about 75–90 words. Use the Threads (500) button above to check your post before publishing.
Does X (Twitter) count spaces and punctuation?
Yes. Every character, including spaces, punctuation and emojis, counts towards the 280-character limit, and links are counted as 23 characters whatever their real length.
How long should an email be?
Shorter usually works better for replies. An analysis of over 40 million emails by the email tool Boomerang found messages of about 50–125 words got the best response rates. Paste your draft above to check its length.
What is the ideal blog post length for SEO?
Google has said there is no ideal word count — pages rank for being helpful, not for being long. Write as much as you need to answer the reader's question fully, then cut anything that doesn't add value.
What is the average reading speed?
Most adults read about 200–250 words per minute silently. This counter uses 200 words per minute for its reading-time estimate. For a detailed breakdown, try our Reading Time Estimator.
