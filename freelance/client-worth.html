What is this Tool?

The Client Lifetime Value (CLV) Calculator helps freelancers understand the true long-term worth of a client relationship — not just a single project fee. When you know a client's lifetime value, you can make better decisions about how much time to invest in acquisition, how aggressively to price retention, and which clients deserve premium service and attention. A client who gives you ₹30,000 per project four times a year for three years — and refers two more clients — is worth far more than their first invoice suggests.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is a good client lifetime value?
A: This varies hugely by industry and rates. The key insight is that LTV is almost always much higher than most freelancers realise — which is why client retention is so much more valuable than constantly chasing new clients.
Q: How do I increase client lifetime value?
A: Deliver excellent work consistently, communicate proactively, offer new services as their business grows, send referral incentives, and check in periodically even when there's no active project.
Q: Is it worth offering discounts for long-term clients?
A: A 10-15% discount for a client who commits to 6+ months of work can be worth it if it increases their lifetime value. However, avoid discounts with clients who never commit to long-term work.
Q: How do I get more referrals?
A: Simply ask. Most satisfied clients are happy to refer but don't think to do it proactively. After delivering great work, ask: 'Do you know anyone else who might benefit from this kind of help?'
💡 Pro Tip: Retaining an existing client costs 5-7x less than acquiring a new one. Before spending time on new client outreach, ask yourself: have I maximised the potential of every existing client relationship?
