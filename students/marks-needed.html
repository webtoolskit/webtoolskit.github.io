What is this Tool?

The Marks Needed Calculator tells you exactly how many marks you need to score in your upcoming final exam to achieve your desired overall percentage or grade. This tool eliminates the uncertainty of 'how much do I need to score?' that students face before final exams. Whether you're aiming to pass, get a first class, distinction, or a specific percentage for scholarship eligibility, this calculator gives you a clear target.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What if the marks needed exceed the maximum?
A: If the calculator shows you need more than the maximum possible marks, your target is no longer achievable with the current scores. Adjust your target to the maximum achievable percentage given your current marks.
Q: Can internal marks compensate for poor finals?
A: In most universities, you need to pass the final exam separately regardless of internal marks. A high internal score helps your aggregate but usually cannot compensate for failing the final.
Q: What is the minimum passing marks in Indian universities?
A: Most universities require 35-40% in each subject to pass. Some professional courses require 45-50%. Always check your specific university's regulations.
Q: How are internal marks typically weighted?
A: In most Indian universities, internals carry 20-30% weightage and finals carry 70-80%. Some universities use 40:60 or 50:50 splits. Check your examination regulations for exact weightage.
💡 Pro Tip: Focus your exam preparation on high-weightage topics first. Most exams follow a pattern where 20% of the syllabus covers 80% of the marks — identify these topics from previous years' papers and master them before moving to lower-weightage areas.
