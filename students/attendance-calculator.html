What is this Tool?

The Attendance Calculator is a free student attendance calculator that helps you track your current attendance percentage and instantly find out how many more classes you can afford to miss. Also known as a class bunk calculator, it is designed specifically for Indian college students where the 75% attendance requirement is mandatory in most universities.

Whether you study at VIT, KLU, Anna University, Mumbai University or any other Indian university — this 75 attendance calculator works for all institutions and all minimum attendance requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a class bunk calculator?
A class bunk calculator helps you find out exactly how many classes you can skip while staying above your college's minimum attendance requirement, usually 75%. Enter your total classes, attended classes and remaining classes to get an instant answer.
How does the 75 attendance calculator work?
Attendance % = (Classes Attended ÷ Total Classes Held) × 100. It then calculates the minimum classes you must attend out of the total (including remaining classes) to stay at or above 75%, and tells you how many you can safely skip.
Does this work for VIT and KLU students?
Yes! This free attendance calculator works for all Indian universities including VIT, KLU, Anna University, Mumbai University, Delhi University, Pune University and any college with a minimum attendance requirement. Just enter your college's specific percentage.
What happens if my attendance falls below 75%?
Most Indian universities debar students with less than 75% attendance from appearing in semester examinations. Some colleges allow relaxation for medical emergencies with valid documentation. Always check your specific college's attendance policy.
Is attendance calculated per subject or overall?
In most Indian universities, attendance is calculated per subject — not as an overall average. You might have excellent overall attendance but be dangerously low in one specific subject. Use this calculator for each subject separately.
💡 Pro Tip: Track your attendance subject-by-subject, not just overall. Most universities calculate eligibility per subject, not in aggregate. Use this class bunk calculator for each subject separately to stay safe in every class!
