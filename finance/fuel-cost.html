What is this Petrol Cost Calculator?

This free petrol cost calculator — also called a journey fuel cost calculator or rough petrol cost calculator — helps you find out exactly how much fuel will cost for any trip. Just enter your distance, vehicle mileage and current fuel price to get an instant result.

The bar chart on the right compares your fuel cost at 6 different mileage levels — so you can instantly see how much you'd save with a more fuel-efficient vehicle for the same journey.

The formula is simple: Fuel Cost = (Distance ÷ Mileage) × Fuel Price. Our calculator does this instantly, with round trip support and monthly cost estimation built in.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate petrol costs for a journey?
The formula to calculate petrol costs is: Fuel Cost = (Distance in km ÷ Vehicle mileage in km/l) × Fuel price per litre. For example, a 100 km journey in a car with 15 km/l mileage at ₹103/litre costs: (100 ÷ 15) × 103 = ₹686.67.
How much petrol for 13 km?
For a 13 km journey, at 15 km/l: 13 ÷ 15 = 0.87 litres = ~₹89.50 at ₹103/litre. At 20 km/l: 0.65 litres = ~₹67. Use the calculator above with your specific mileage for an exact result.
How to work out how much petrol will cost for a journey?
Find the distance on Google Maps, note your car's mileage, check the current petrol price, then use: Cost = (Distance ÷ Mileage) × Price. Or enter these three numbers above for an instant result with a visual comparison chart.
Are petrol prices the same across India?
No — petrol and diesel prices vary significantly by state due to different VAT and cess rates. Always check the current price at your local petrol pump before planning a long trip.
How do I calculate petrol money to split among passengers?
Calculate the total petrol cost using this calculator, then divide by the number of passengers. For example, ₹1,200 total fuel cost among 4 people = ₹300 each.
