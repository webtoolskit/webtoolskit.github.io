What is this Tool?

The Pregnancy Due Date Calculator estimates your baby's expected arrival date based on the first day of your last menstrual period (LMP) and your average cycle length. Pregnancy is typically 40 weeks (280 days) from the first day of your LMP. This calculator uses Naegele's rule — the standard medical method used by obstetricians worldwide — adjusted for your specific cycle length.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How accurate is this due date?
A: The estimated due date (EDD) is accurate to within 1-2 weeks for most pregnancies. An ultrasound in the first trimester provides a more precise date. Only 5% of babies arrive on their exact EDD.
Q: What are the three trimesters?
A: First trimester: weeks 1-13. Second trimester: weeks 14-27. Third trimester: weeks 28-40+. Each trimester brings distinct developmental milestones for the baby.
Q: When should I see a doctor?
A: Schedule your first prenatal appointment as soon as you confirm pregnancy, ideally by week 8. Early prenatal care is essential for a healthy pregnancy outcome.
Q: What if my cycle is not 28 days?
A: This calculator adjusts for cycle lengths other than 28 days. If your cycle is longer, ovulation (and thus conception) happens later, pushing the due date forward accordingly.
💡 Pro Tip: Always confirm your due date with your gynaecologist through an early ultrasound scan, especially if your cycle is irregular. An ultrasound between weeks 8-12 is the most accurate way to date a pregnancy.
